## Key findings

1. **The "Mutual Top-1" filter is the single best portfolio rule we have.**
   When ET *and* SARR independently pick the same horse as #1, hit rate jumps
   from ~23% to **33.3%** and ROI rises to **+26.9%**. This is the strongest
   quantitative case for the Mutual Top Picks section already shown on the
   dashboard overview.

2. **WIN > PLACE at these stakes.** Both PLACE strategies lose money; place
   dividends are too compressed to cover the lower-probability hits. The
   "SARR PLACE hits 53.6%" headline number is misleading as a betting rule.

3. **ET beats SARR on WIN in aggregate** (+20.3% vs +2.9% ROI) — driven
   mainly by the Apr-15 HV-mile meeting (+$2,055 ET vs −$5 SARR). Consistent
   with the venue-bucket analysis in `reports/et_vs_sarr_analysis.md`.

4. **QIN-box of mutual top-2 is a trap.** Only 2 hits in 34 meetings; the
   two horses agreed on by both models are too correlated with the
   market-favourite pair, so QIN dividends don't cover the bet cost.

5. **User's actual Apr-22 bets ran −58% ROI on a 20-ticket sample** (HV,
   mostly QIN/QPL boxes). Pure model baseline (MUTUAL_WIN_TOP1) that day
   returned +$170 on $200 staked (+85%). Small n, but worth logging.
